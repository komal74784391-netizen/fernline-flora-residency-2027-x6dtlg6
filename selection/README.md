# Spring 2027 Illustrated Flora Residency — Selection Cycle

**Studio:** Fernline Studio  
**Cycle:** Spring 2027 Illustrated Flora Residency  
**Applications received:** 20

## Selection rules applied

1. **Deadline** — applications received after 10 November 2026, 23:59 US Eastern were treated as late and rejected.
2. **Portfolio** — a link to recent work was required; applications without one were rejected.
3. **Experience** — applicants had to state their years of professional illustration experience; applications without a stated figure were rejected.
4. **Availability** — applicants had to be available for the whole residency window, 3 May – 25 June 2027; partial availability was rejected.
5. **Discipline** — only botanical illustration and scientific illustration were eligible; any other primary discipline was rejected.
6. **Ranking** — eligible applicants were ranked by years of professional experience (highest first); the top six were invited to interview and the remaining eligible applicants form the waitlist.
